Middle-aged male with uncontrolled type 2 diabetes and hypertension has persistent hyperglycemia (HbA1c 9.1%), elevated LDL, and positive microalbuminuria with blood pressure above target. Symptoms include fatigue, nocturia, and blurry vision, with poor medication adherence and high-risk lifestyle factors. Clinical priority is tighter glycemic and blood-pressure control, cardiometabolic risk reduction, nutrition and exercise intervention, and close endocrine follow-up.
