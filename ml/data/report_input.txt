Patient is a 56-year-old male presenting with poorly controlled type 2 diabetes and hypertension. Over the last three months, he reports increased fatigue, nocturia, and intermittent blurry vision. Fasting glucose values at home ranged from 165 to 220 mg/dL. Recent lab panel shows HbA1c 9.1%, LDL 142 mg/dL, creatinine 1.0 mg/dL, and microalbuminuria positive. Blood pressure in clinic was 154/94 mmHg on repeated measurements. Physical exam notes central obesity, mild pedal edema, and no focal neurologic deficits. Current medications include metformin but adherence is inconsistent due to missed evening doses. Dietary history reveals frequent sugary beverages and high refined carbohydrate intake. Patient has sedentary lifestyle and sleeps less than six hours nightly due to shift work. Plan discussed includes glycemic control reinforcement, cardiovascular risk reduction, dietary counseling, activity targets, and close follow-up with endocrinology and primary care.
